# Green Computing: A Bibliometric Analysis of Energy Efficiency and Sustainability

## Description

This repository provides supplementary datasets and analytical results supporting a bibliometric study of Green Computing research published between 1995 and 2025.

The study examines 2,994 articles and reviews retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection, combining bibliometric performance analysis, H-Classics, and longitudinal science mapping using SciMAT v1.1.06.

## Repository Contents

### SciMAT Results

**`Report SciMAT/`**

Contains analytical reports generated using SciMAT, supporting the identification, strategic positioning, bibliometric performance, and thematic evolution of research themes across three periods: 1995–2015, 2016–2020, and 2021–2025.

### Search Strategy Validation

**`GC_341_RelevanceAssessment.csv`**

Contains the thematic relevance assessment of a random sample of 341 publications selected from the original corpus of 2,994 records. Includes bibliographic information, publication identifiers, and relevance classifications. Of the evaluated publications, 327 were classified as relevant and 14 as non-relevant.

### Bibliometric Performance Data

**`GC_most_productive_authors.csv`**

Lists the most productive authors, including publication counts and associated Web of Science research areas.

**`GC_most_cited_authors.csv`**

Presents the most cited authors, including citation counts, publication counts, research areas, and publication years.

**`GC_most_productive_countries.csv`**

Reports publication counts and percentages for the leading contributing countries, using the full-counting method.

**`GC_wos_subject_categories_and_research_areas.csv`**

Contains publication counts and percentages for the leading Web of Science Subject Categories and Research Areas.

**`GC_most_productive_journals.csv`**

Lists the most productive journals, including publication counts, accumulated citations, and journal impact factors.

## Data Source and Methodology

- **Database:** Web of Science Core Collection
- **Data retrieval date:** 13 February 2026
- **Publication period:** 1995–2025
- **Corpus:** 2,994 articles and reviews
- **Analysis software:** SciMAT v1.1.06
- **Methods:** Bibliometric performance analysis, H-Classics, and longitudinal science mapping

## Data Availability

The datasets and analytical results are provided as supplementary materials to facilitate the examination, verification, and reuse of the bibliometric findings presented in the associated manuscript.
